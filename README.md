## About Me

Hi! I'm a recent graduate with a Master's degree in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics.

My background combines biology, programming, and biological data analysis.

During my Master's studies, I gained research experience in two areas of bioinformatics:

- **Environmental DNA metabarcoding at INRAE (UMR DYNAFOR):** I worked with Illumina sequencing data to study wild pollinators, using sequence quality control, clustering, and taxonomic assignment methods.
- **Single-cell transcriptomics at INSERM:** I studied the locus-specific expression of transposable elements in follicular lymphoma using scRNA-seq data and bioinformatics workflows.

These experiences helped me develop skills in biological data analysis, next-generation sequencing (NGS), and computational workflows.

I am currently strengthening my Python, R, and Git skills through practical projects.

My goal is to work as a bioinformatics engineer and contribute to research in genomics and computational biology.
